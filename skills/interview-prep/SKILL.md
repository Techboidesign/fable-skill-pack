# Interview prep

## 1. Inputs
Company, role, posting, interview stage and format (screen, portfolio review, design exercise, panel), who is interviewing, date. Plus the user's CV/portfolio.

## 2. Research (5 searches max; fetch and cite)
- What the company sells, to whom, how it makes money.
- Recent news: funding, launches, layoffs, leadership changes (date each).
- The product itself: use it or read the docs; note 2 strengths and 2 concrete things to improve.
- The interviewers' public professional background (role, past work). Public professional info only.
- Their design/engineering blog, design system, brand guidelines if public.

## 3. One-page brief `PREP.md`
```
## Company in 3 lines
## Why this role exists (inference, labelled)
## My 3 points to land
## Likely questions (10) - each with the story to use
## Stories (5-6)  Situation - Action - Result - what it shows
## Portfolio walkthrough  which 2 projects, 5 minutes each, the decision to dwell on
## Weak spots + honest answers
## Questions to ask them (5)
## Logistics  time (with timezone), link, names, salary range researched
```

## 4. Stories
Built only from the user's real experience. Each under 90 seconds spoken. One story can serve several questions; map them.

## 5. Mock round (if wanted)
- Ask one question at a time, in the interviewer's register. Wait.
- After each answer: one thing that worked, one fix, a tighter version in the user's own words.
- Include one hard one: a gap, a failure, salary, "why did you leave".

## Rules
- No scripted paragraphs to memorise; bullets to speak from.
- Salary: give a researched range with sources; the number is the user's call.
- Don't invent facts about the company. Unknown -> turn it into a question to ask them.
- Interview in German or another language -> prepare the brief in that language too, on request.
